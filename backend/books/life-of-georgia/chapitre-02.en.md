# Chapter 2: The Rape

When she arrived, she saw Jordan at the table and cried out: "Is it lunchtime already?"

"Of course," said Jordan, "we always have lunch at twelve o'clock sharp."

At that, she rushed over to eat the dish of the day. On the menu there was: vegetable salad for the starter, tomato-sauce rice with mutton for the main course, and an apple-scented king's cake for dessert.

Little Georgia did not know that behind this handsome-boy mask there was a young prince who had lost his parents and had, over time, become a psychopath. Georgia settled properly at the table, picked up a fork and a table knife, and served herself. In about thirty minutes she devoured the whole meal. Of course everyone had eaten, but she had taken more than the others.

Before the food was eaten by Georgia, there had been a brief moment when she had gone to the bathroom. During that time, the prince was plotting against her: he wanted to rape her. But, naive, she suspected nothing. In the food there was poison: "koto-nitrate 2," a serum that puts one to sleep in a matter of minutes. Jordan had used it to knock Georgia out and abuse her.

Some time later, Georgia slowly regained consciousness in Jordan's room.

"Where am I? Why am I tied up? Aaahh, it hurts..."

Georgia opened her eyes and saw Jordan leaning over her. She said: "What are you doing?"

Jordan answered: "But I love you, Georgia."

Understanding what he had done to her, Georgia began to scream and call for help, but unfortunately she was in an isolated room of the castle. Jordan did not stop, deaf to her pleas. She wept, remembering what her father had told her: "Never trust a man's beauty."
