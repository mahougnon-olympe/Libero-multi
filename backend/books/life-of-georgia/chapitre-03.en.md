# Chapter 3: Revenge

...a commotion. The guard was in fact looking for the prince to announce the arrival of his aunt. Hearing this, the prince took fright and quickly got dressed again. Georgia was relieved, but she was still tied to the bed. Just in time, the guard had the curiosity to check the room: he saw her, asked her a few questions, and pledged to help Georgia stop Jordan.

On Jordan's side, at three o'clock:

"Uh... good evening, Aunt Mia... what are you doing here... right now?" said Jordan.

"I came to greet you, my little prince," the aunt answered. "It's true that ever since your parents died, you no longer speak to anyone," Aunt Mia went on.

"Yes, that's true, Aunt Mia, you're right," said Jordan.

"I have an idea: how about we go get some fresh air?" said Aunt Mia.

"Why not," Jordan replied.

As the two of them headed toward the exit, they saw a woman half knocked out. It was indeed little Georgia. The aunt, startled, exclaimed: "But who is this?"

Jordan answered hesitantly: "S... She's a girl from the village, her name is Georgia."

The aunt went on: "What is she doing here?"

Jordan answered: "I... I don't think I know!?"

The aunt shouted: "You don't think you know!!! You're telling me you don't think you know!!! You bring a village girl home and you tell me you don't think you know!!! Have you gone completely mad? Why did you bring her to the castle?"

Jordan answered timidly: "I... I felt like making love."

Aunt Mia replied: "Ohh, really, this child... Teenagers these days! Your father was right to want to send you to the villagers' boarding school."

On her way out of the castle, the aunt spotted the guard who had saved Georgia. He told her everything Georgia had said to him. The aunt listened carefully, then said to herself: "Don't worry, Jordan, soon I'll speak with my lawyer to send you back to boarding school..."

After these words, the aunt left. Georgia, still in the room, unconscious, had a dream: a dream in which she saw herself receive a number of powers from a spirit. Indeed, this girl came from a family of supers. She was the youngest, and it happened that her powers had been slow to awaken. It was finally at that moment that Georgia felt a force rise within her: her brown, silky hair began to shine. These were her powers.

Jordan, coming back toward the room, saw a glow. "What is that!!!" he exclaimed. He came closer to see what was happening, and there was Georgia, floating in the air with the grace of a goddess. Marveling but frightened by what he saw, he ran to fetch a weapon from the guards' camp. Georgia's anger blazed higher and higher. Jordan reached the guards' den, took an axe, and retraced his steps. Seeing him, Georgia hurled a fireball at him; trying to dodge it, he dropped the weapon, which took the blow and melted.

Far from there, in a monstrous chamber, was a magician working on a potion of indestructibility. Jordan needed it: he ran to fetch the potion and swallowed the whole vial. He became indestructible. He came back to Georgia and began to throw things at her while laughing slyly, but it did almost nothing to Georgia.

You should know that a month earlier, Jordan had been subjected to the experiments of a mad scientist. The scientist had no subject to exploit, so he had jumped at the first opportunity, and that was when Prince Jordan happened to pass by. From consulting the gods, it turned out that the prince would only be saved the moment he drank a little water from the fairy lake. This story became known to Georgia only when she laid her hand on Jordan's forehead. The tension subsided, and the quest began.
