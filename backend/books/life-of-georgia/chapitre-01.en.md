# Chapter 1: The Arrival of Georgia

Once upon a time there was a beautiful girl, wise and clever, named Georgia, who lived with her family in a distant village called Aboula. In her family's culture, it was expected that one marry from a young age, but she wanted to meet the love of her life for herself. So one day she left the village, to the great despair of her parents, who worried for her.

Some time later, Georgia found herself in a town she did not know: Black-Fish. Lost, she asked for directions to get her bearings. It was then that she crossed paths with a young man who caught her eye: the build of a sports coach, white teeth, a smile that revealed a reassuring face. Nor did she go unnoticed by him.

He said to her: "My name is Jordan. What are you doing here? Come to my villa."

She answered, hesitant: "Uh... yes... maybe."

Once at the villa, she asked to take a bath. Jordan showed her the first bathroom, which was on the second floor. After that answer she headed upstairs. Having taken her bath, she did not know what to wear. So she came down, a towel around her hips, to ask Jordan whether he might have any women's clothes, when suddenly three guards who did not know Georgia appeared in her path. In such a case, the guards were permitted to arrest intruders. Georgia, startled, said:

"I came here with Jordan."

One of the guards stammered: "J... Jordan... the prince!"

"What? Jordan is the prince?" Georgia wondered.

"Yes!" exclaimed the guard. "You didn't know?"

"Uh... no," said Georgia.

Then she went to Jordan and said: "You're a prince, and why didn't you tell me before? This is crazy! By the way, my clothes," said Georgia, "ah yes, the clothes..."

"Uh, you can find some women's clothes in suite 22, on the third floor."

"Yes, I'm going."

Georgia went, took some clothes and came back to the living room.
